          Destinations

          People, groups or channels you can send to. Pick one from the To menu on the Send tab.

          Don't know it? Send any message to your bot in Telegram (or add the bot to a group and post there), then tap Find.

            Passcode lock

            Encrypts your bot token in this browser and asks for a passcode when the app opens. Your messages, history and links are hidden behind the lock screen but are not encrypted.

            The token is still written in the CONFIG block at the top of this file. Delete it there, or it stays readable by anyone who opens the file.

            Automation

            Open this page with a link to prefill a message from a bookmark, an iPhone Shortcut or Tasker. Options: text, to (destination name), mode=html, silent=1, nopreview=1. It never sends by itself.

            Backup and restore

            Links, templates and destinations. Your bot token is never included.

            This browser is blocking local storage, so settings, links and history will be lost when you close the page.

            Your token is stored only in this browser and is sent only to api.telegram.org. Anyone who can open this page on your device can read it unless you turn on the passcode lock, so keep this file private.
